One sentence a house cat would say in its own defence, five seconds after sweeping a glass off the table — no apology, no confession, just the most convincing case a cat can make for itself.

## The entry

> It had been squatting on my sunbeam since Tuesday, so I served the glass an eviction notice — one paw, no warning, no appeal.

## Why it works

- **One sentence, 125 characters** — a single spoken line, not a paragraph.
- **The cat's own logic** — the glass is a squatter on *its* sunbeam; the cat is the landlord, not the culprit. No apology, no remorse.
- **Fresh idea** — an eviction, not gravity, a slippery edge, or "it was already falling".
- **Concrete detail + closing twist** — "since Tuesday" makes the tenancy real; "no warning, no appeal" lands the joke in the final words.
- **Three beats** — one paw, no warning, no appeal: action, process, verdict.
